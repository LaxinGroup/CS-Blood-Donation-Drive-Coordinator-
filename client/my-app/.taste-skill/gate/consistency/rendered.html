Do I need an account to book?

Yes. Create a donor account with your campus email, then book any open slot.

What if two donors book the last slot?

The first booking the database records gets it. The other donor is told the slot just filled and picks another time.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, from your donor page. The place goes straight back to the drive for someone else.

Where do I find my booking code?

On the confirmation and on your donor page. It starts with BDC; show it at the check-in desk.

When is my next donation date?

56 days after your last donation. Your donor page shows the exact date.

Who can give blood, and how often

The booking form asks five questions before it shows a time. If one rule stops you today, it tells you which one and the date you can come back.

  • Age16 or older
  • WeightAt least 50 kg
  • Since your last donation56 days
  • After a tattoo or piercing3 months
  • After a course of antibiotics48 hours
